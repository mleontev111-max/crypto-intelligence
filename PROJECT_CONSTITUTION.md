# PROJECT CONSTITUTION

1. The project does not promise certainty or guaranteed market outperformance.
2. A published forecast is immutable. Corrections create a new record/version; they never rewrite history.
3. RAW source payloads are immutable and retained so historical knowledge can be reconstructed point-in-time.
4. Quant/statistical models own numerical probabilities. LLMs may classify, summarize, contextualize, and explain, but must not silently overwrite quant outputs.
5. Correlation, attribution, and causality are distinct. Explanations of market moves must state uncertainty and evidence strength.
6. Every model/recommendation must identify model version, forecast horizon, timestamp, and data snapshot.
7. New models enter as challengers. Promotion requires predefined out-of-sample and shadow evaluation against baselines and the current champion.
8. Evaluation must include calibration and risk, not hit rate alone.
9. Backtests must guard against look-ahead bias, leakage, survivorship bias, revision bias, and cherry-picking.
10. Phase 0-5 permit research and paper portfolios only. No autonomous live-money execution.
11. Simplicity wins: a complex model that does not beat a simple baseline is rejected.
12. GitHub canonical state and checkpoints are the continuity source of truth; old chats are not.
